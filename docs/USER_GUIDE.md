# 📖 ScamShield India — Simple User Guide

*For everyone — parents, grandparents, students, shopkeepers.*

## How to check a message (3 steps)

1. **Copy** the message you are unsure about (long-press it in SMS or WhatsApp → Copy).
2. **Open ScamShield India** and **paste** it into the big box.
3. Tap **Check Message**.

## What the colours mean

| Colour | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Red — High risk** | Very likely a scam | Do **not** click, pay, call or reply. Delete it. |
| 🟠 **Orange — Medium risk** | Several warning signs | Stop. Verify using the official app or website. |
| 🟡 **Yellow — Low risk** | A small warning sign | Be careful; check who sent it. |
| 🟢 **Green — Safe** | No common scam signs | Still stay alert with unknown senders. |

Words shown in **red highlight** are the warning signs. Read the "What you should do" list below the result.

## 5 golden rules

1. 🔐 **Never share OTP, UPI PIN, CVV or passwords** — with anyone, ever.
2. 📷 **Scanning a QR code only SENDS money.** You never need to scan or enter a PIN to receive money.
3. 🏦 **Banks never ask you to update KYC through an SMS link.**
4. 🚔 **There is no such thing as "digital arrest".** Police do not arrest people over video calls.
5. ⏰ **Urgency is a trick.** If a message rushes you, slow down.

## If you already lost money

- ☎️ Call **1930** immediately (the first hours matter most)
- 🌐 Report at **cybercrime.gov.in**
- 🏦 Call your bank's official helpline to block your card/UPI
- 📵 Report the number at **sancharsaathi.gov.in** (Chakshu)

## Your privacy

ScamShield works **inside your phone's browser**. The message you paste is **never sent anywhere** and is **not saved**. It even works without internet once opened.
